About me

I bring more than 15 years of experience in brand design, digital marketing, and web design. I create brand identities and campaign graphics, design and build websites, and manage marketing campaigns and social media accounts to grow audiences.

I sold my first piece of art before I turned ten. By twelve I was teaching myself to build websites on my first computer and marketing my first business, a travel agency called Princess Tours. I’ve been building brands ever since.

I trained in hyperrealistic drawing and earned my degree in business management with a concentration in marketing, so I think like an artist and plan like a strategist. That is how I take a brand from first sketch to sold-out launch, with creative approved by Hennessy, Belvedere, Red Bull, Maker’s Mark and Rivian, and campaigns for DJs like DJ Hunny Bee, Jae Murphy, DJ Puffy and DJ Umami.

I’m also endlessly curious about technology, and I treat new tools as part of my creative kit. It’s why I design and code my own sites, and why I built OTT Studio with AI when no marketing platform on the market did what I needed.

OTT Studio, designed and built by me

AI tools

Why I built it. None of the marketing tools out there did everything I needed, and juggling a stack of apps still left gaps. I love how the Apple ecosystem just works, with everything connected in one place, and I wanted that for marketing: one tool where I can see everything, live. So I designed and built it myself.

experienceott.github.io/marketing
